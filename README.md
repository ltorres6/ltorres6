## <img src="https://media.giphy.com/media/VgCDAzcKvsR6OM0uWg/giphy.gif" width="50"> About Me

Senior Scientific Solutions Engineer at Flywheel, where I help pharma and academic research teams turn their imaging questions into automated pipelines: curation, QC, scan classification, de-identification, segmentation and quantitative analysis across MR, CT and PET. These days that also means deep-learning models and a growing pile of LLM tooling. I hold a PhD in Medical Physics from the University of Wisconsin-Madison, where I specialized in advanced MRI techniques for lung imaging (and learned to love GPUs).

Originally from New Mexico and now based in Chapel Hill, North Carolina, I have a passion for exploring the intersection of technology and healthcare. Off the clock you'll find me tinkering with my homelab and 3D printing.

## <img src="https://media.giphy.com/media/LnQjpWaON8nhr21vNW/giphy.gif" width="30"> Current Work

- **Medical imaging pipelines**: turning research questions into automated workflows for MR, CT and PET
- **Scan classification from physics**: figuring out what a series actually is from how it was acquired, not what someone named it (fw-classification, ISMRM 2024)
- **AI/ML in healthcare**: 3D segmentation models in research pipelines, and fine-tuning vision-language foundation models for imaging
- **LLM solutions**: RAG documentation assistants, agent pipelines in CI, and MCP servers
- **Open source with [OSIPI](https://osipi.ismrm.org/)** (Open Science Initiative for Perfusion Imaging):
    - Architect of [osipy](https://github.com/OSIPI/osipy), OSIPI's Python package for perfusion MRI
    - Task force lead for the Python package and for the website & technical support
    - Google Summer of Code mentor (2024, 2025)
- **Around here**: [pydemons](https://github.com/ltorres6/pydemons) (GPU demons registration on SigPy) and [imoco](https://github.com/ltorres6/imoco) (a Python version of iMoCo, the motion-compensated recon by Xucheng Zhu and the [PulmonaryMRI](https://github.com/PulmonaryMRI/imoco_recon) group)
